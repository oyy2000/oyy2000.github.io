Research Experience

    Raleigh, U.S.A

    StepBack: Step-Level Error-Localized Resampling for Efficient LLM Reasoning
    In Proceedings of NeurIPS 2026
    • Proposed StepBack, a training-free inference-time decoding method that improves self-consistency by localizing likely first-error positions in chain-of-thought trajectories and resampling only uncertain suffixes instead of regenerating full reasoning paths.
    • Designed lightweight step-level rollback signals using NLL increases and process reward model score drops to allocate test-time compute more efficiently within reasoning trajectories.
    • Evaluated across math reasoning and multi-hop QA benchmarks; the PRM-based variant improved accuracy on 10 / 14 model-dataset pairs while reducing tokens in all settings, with up to 39.8% token savings over SC@32.
  • Jul 2026 -
    Sep 2026

    Shanghai, China

    Large-Scale Elastic Continued Pretraining for MoE Models
    Project
    • Adapted elastic training to a closed-source 15B-A2B MoE model using 1,024 GPUs, enabling three nested, weight-sharing subnetworks (3B, 9B, 15B) for different resource budgets.
    • Explored five architectural dimensions (MoE depth, expert width, expert count, attention heads, and top-k routing) through budget-constrained sampling and importance-guided pruning, constructing nested subnetworks under joint total-parameter and active-parameter constraints.
    • Optimized shared-weight continued pretraining across 11 benchmarks, outperforming size- and training-budget-matched standalone models by 1.6 and 3.9 points for small and large subnetworks, respectively.
  • Aug 2025 -

    Raleigh, U.S.A

    DenseSteer: Steering Small Language Models towards Dense Math Reasoning
    In Proceedings of ICML 2026
    • Proposed Dense Reasoning as a structural property of strong mathematical reasoning, empirically showing that higher reasoning density (fewer steps, more tokens per step) correlates with higher accuracy on GSM8K and OOD benchmarks.
    • Developed a training-free, inference-time activation steering framework using only 50 rewrite-based contrastive pairs, avoiding distribution shift and preserving low token-level NLL.
    • Achieved up to +5% GSM8K, +17.5% AMC, and +10% AIME accuracy gains on Qwen-2.5-3B-Instruct and LLaMA-3.2-3B-Instruct compared with heavy training-based distillation methods.
